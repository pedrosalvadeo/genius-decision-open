03 / HOW GENIUS DECIDES

From a question
to the best decision.

An AI can draft the model of a decision, but it does not choose. The Genius kernel computes every route in every scenario. Here it is, step by step, with Earth's climate.

CO₂ emitted, within budgetbudget exceeded: life is lost2 °C budget

Data: the Earth twin 2025–2105. 2 °C budgets from IPCC AR6, 42.2 GtCO₂ emitted in 2025 (Global Carbon Budget 2025), one third for each scenario because the IPCC gives no likelihoods. It only includes climate and forests; with the other crossed boundaries, the PVS can only be equal or lower.

04 / THE FIRST PRODUCT

Decide within
the nine boundaries.

A decision report for companies and organizations: every route of a decision, in every scenario, against the planetary boundaries.

    A supplier, a plant, a product, a goal.

    You describe the decision in your words. Genius returns which routes keep life, where each one leaves each boundary, and what is best for the company, its workers, the community and nature.

    1. 1An AI drafts the model: routes, scenarios and everyone affected, with each assumed number marked as a hypothesis.
    2. 2The kernel computes every route in every scenario: life condition, PVS and the best route for each one affected.
    3. 3You get a printable report, with the laws that apply and every hypothesis editable.
