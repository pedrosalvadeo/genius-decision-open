GENIUS FOR COMPANIES · DECISION REPORT

Decide within the planetary boundaries.

Earth has crossed 7 of the 9 planetary boundaries. Every company decision widens or narrows the room left in each one. This report shows, before you decide, which route keeps life and leaves the most room, and which laws already ask you to assess it.

Describe a decision your company has to make. The AI only translates it into a model with routes, scenarios, the people affected and the living systems it touches; the Genius kernel calculates every route in every scenario. You get which routes keep the life condition, their PVS, how each route leaves each planetary boundary and what is best for each affected party, with every assumed number marked as a hypothesis you can correct.
