GENIUS LAB

Where Genius learns.

The cases of water, blood and Earth's climate are a handful of decisions among millions, in a few domains. Genius has not yet reached domains at scale. Here are those first cases, the dynamic twins, the eight modules and the tools for AI agents and developers. They are early, and close to the code.

01 / HOW WE GET THERE

Change how decisions
are made, and which ones.

The goals won't be reached with one big decision, but with millions of small and large decisions made differently. This is the path, step by step.

  1. 01

    Calculate, don't opine

    An AI can draft the model of a decision, but it doesn't choose. The kernel goes through every route in every scenario with exact numbers: which route preserves life, its probability of sustaining it (PVS) and what each affected party gains or loses, without adding criteria together.

    IN USE
  2. 02

    Bring it to every decision

    Simple products for people, organizations and AI agents, from an everyday question to a river basin, a blood bank or the planet.

    IN BETA
  3. 03

    Check against reality

    Predictions are frozen and compared with what happens. On the 2nd and 17th of every month, the published readings for Denver, Mendoza and Lake Mead are recorded.

    IN USE
  4. 04

    Learn and evolve

    A neural network learns from every kernel verdict and proposes better routes; the kernel judges all of them. Living populations of routes evolve every day in every twin.

    EXPERIMENTAL
  5. 05

    Connect the twins

    One twin's decision is another twin's scenario, like Denver and Lake Powell on the Colorado River. Twins of twins, up to a noosphere of dynamic twins of the whole planet.

    RESEARCH

The planet's climate and forests

The CO2 budget for staying under 2 °C, deforestation and four routes for humanity. Continuing current policies has PVS = 0 in all three climate scenarios; reaching zero by 2045 preserves the life condition in all of them.

04 / GENIUS MATHEMATICS

Giving structure
to deep questions.

A framework for representing systems, exploring futures and comparing decisions by their functioning, generative capacity, wellbeing and conditions for sustained life.

The model declares what it observes, whom it includes and for how long. From resources, relationships, actions and scenarios, it builds trajectories and keeps the evidence behind each conclusion.

Generative capacity (CAG) describes which organizations can be reached, preserved or accumulated. The Probability of Sustained Life (PVS) evaluates a defined viability task, when an adequate probabilistic model and evidence exist.

0 Representation +

Defines states, histories and information. Establishes what can be simplified without losing what a query needs.

I Functionality +

Describes comparable functions and profiles, with shared references and scales.

II Multiscale +

Relates systems and subsystems, keeps their dependencies and accounts for shared resources.

III Generative capacity +

Distinguishes origin, generation, preservation and accumulation. Compares possible and robust repertoires.

IV Wellbeing +

Records the wellbeing dimensions of its carriers over time, with explicit comparison rules.

V Futures +

Builds scenarios and trajectories from actions, available information and declared causal relationships.

VI Evidence and PVS +

Links meaning, fidelity, uncertainty and probability. Checks under which conditions sustained life can be evaluated.

VII Decision +

Applies constraints, compares alternatives and keeps the frontier of options no other beats. Selecting requires a declared rule.

FOUR ESSENTIAL DISTINCTIONS

Clarity before
false certainty.

  • Reality and representation. Every model has a boundary and assumptions.
  • Possibility and probability. An imaginable future needs support before it receives a probability.
  • Capacity and wellbeing. Having more possibilities requires evaluating what effects they produce, and for whom.
  • Unknown and zero. Missing information is kept as missing; it may prevent a conclusion.

05 / HOMO GENIUS

A way of thinking.
A direction for action.

Homo Genius is a philosophical proposal by Pedro Salvadeo: direct intelligence toward protecting life, enriching its diversity and widening the possibilities for wellbeing.

I

Reality is a dynamic network

People, organisms, resources and environments are related. An action transforms one's own conditions and those of others. Understanding those relationships helps recognize their consequences.

II

Wellbeing is possibility

In this vision, wellbeing is the present possibility of improving the conditions for life. It includes material resources, relationships, time, information and the capacity to act.

III

The whole path matters

A decision opens some futures and closes others. Evaluating it means looking at whom it affects, what happens along the way and what possibilities it leaves open afterward.

IV

Learning transforms action

Observing results lets us revise our representation of the world. Genius aims to adapt its models and strategies with evidence, keeping the memory of what it has learned.

↳

The philosophy proposes a direction. The mathematics sharpens the questions. Experience must show what works and under which conditions.
