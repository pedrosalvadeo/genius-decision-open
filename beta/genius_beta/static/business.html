Bring the
nine checks

Earth has crossed 7 of the nine planetary boundaries that keep it habitable. They were crossed one decision at a time, and that is how they come back: Genius calculates each company decision route by route, so the one that leaves the most room for life can be chosen before deciding.

01 / THE NINE CHECKS

7 of 9 crossed.
The goal is zero.

Each boundary, its state today and what a company decision moves in it. Planetary Health Check 2025 (Potsdam Institute for Climate Impact Research).

CROSSED

Climate change

Emissions from energy, transport, processes and the supply chain.

CROSSED

Biosphere integrity

Loss of species and habitats from expansion, extraction and pollution.

CROSSED

Land-system change

Deforestation and land conversion for crops, cattle, mining or construction.

CROSSED

Freshwater change

Irrigation, industrial use and discharges into rivers, aquifers and soils.

CROSSED

Nitrogen and phosphorus flows

Fertilisers and effluents that reach rivers, lakes and seas.

CROSSED

Novel entities

Plastics, chemicals and packaging the environment cannot absorb.

CROSSED

Ocean acidification

The emitted CO2 that the ocean absorbs.

SAFE ZONE

Atmospheric aerosol loading

Particles from combustion, burning and dust.

SAFE ZONE

Stratospheric ozone depletion

Refrigerants and gases that destroy ozone.

How Genius helps bring them down

  1. 01

    Measure the room each route leaves

    Each report finds the boundaries a decision touches and calculates where every route leaves them, at the end and at the worst moment, in every scenario.

    PER DECISION
  2. 02

    Drop the routes that lose life

    A route that breaks the life condition of people or living systems in any scenario is flagged first, however profitable it looks.

    FIRST FILTER
  3. 03

    Show the route that leaves the most room, and who pays

    Among the routes that keep life, the report shows which ones leave each boundary with the most room and what each affected party gains or loses, without adding them into one score.

    NO SUMS
  4. 04

    Add decisions up, and check against reality

    A boundary is crossed by millions of decisions and comes back the same way. Calculated decisions are followed against what happens, and the Earth twin measures the progress: today it covers climate and forests.

02 / THE LAW ALREADY ASKS

Assessing impacts
is no longer optional.

These frameworks ask companies to identify their impacts on the boundaries, often with scenario analysis. Genius gives that analysis a calculation: routes, scenarios, everyone affected and every hypothesis in view.

03 / THE REPORT

What your team
gets for each decision.

Read from the kernel and never added into a single score.

  1. 01

    Life and PVS for every route and scenario

    Which routes keep the life condition of the people and living systems affected in every scenario, and their probability of sustained life (PVS).

    FIRST FILTER
  2. 02

    The nine boundaries, and the ones this decision touches

    Where each route leaves each boundary the decision touches, and which routes leave the most room.

    9 CHECKS
  3. 03

    What is best for each affected party

    The company, its workers, the community and nature each get their best routes. When they disagree, the report shows the conflict.

    NO SUMS
  4. 04

    The laws that apply

    For each boundary the decision touches, the frameworks that already ask companies to assess it, so the report can feed their analysis.

    COMPLIANCE
  5. 05

    Every hypothesis in view, and editable

    Every number the AI had to assume is marked. Your team corrects it with real data and the kernel recalculates in seconds, without the AI. The report prints or saves as PDF.
