GENIUS FOR COMPANIES · PUBLIC BETA

Company decisions
within the
planetary boundaries.

Companies measure their impact after deciding, for the report. Genius calculates it before: every route of a decision in every scenario, with the people and living systems it affects and the planetary boundaries it presses.

Earth has already crossed seven of the nine planetary boundaries (Planetary Health Check 2025). Every large decision either widens or narrows the room that is left.

01 / THE REPORT

What your team
gets for each decision.

Four answers, read from the kernel and never added into a single score.

  1. 01

    Life and PVS for every route and scenario

    Which routes keep the life condition of the people and living systems affected in every scenario, and their probability of sustained life (PVS). A route that loses life in some scenario is flagged before anything else.

    FIRST FILTER
  2. 02

    The planetary boundaries each route presses

    Climate, freshwater, land, biosphere and the other boundaries the decision touches: where each route leaves them at the end of the horizon and at its worst moment, and which routes leave the most room.

    9 BOUNDARIES
  3. 03

    What is best for each affected party

    The company, its workers, the community and nature each get their best routes. When they disagree, the report shows the conflict instead of hiding it in a single score.

    NO SUMS
  4. 04

    Every hypothesis in view, and editable

    Every number the AI had to assume is marked. Your team corrects it with real data and the kernel recalculates in seconds, without the AI. The report prints or saves as PDF.

    AUDITABLE

02 / HOW IT WORKS

From a description
to a calculated decision.

The same kernel and eight modules behind the water, blood and Earth twins.

  1. 01

    Describe the decision

    The company, where it operates, the decision and the options. Plain language is enough.

    2 MINUTES
  2. 02

    The AI drafts the model; the kernel calculates

    Routes, scenarios, the people and living systems affected and the boundary stocks. The AI never chooses: the Genius kernel evaluates every route in every scenario with exact numbers.

    ABOUT 1 MINUTE
  3. 03

    Correct, recalculate, decide

    Replace hypotheses with your data, recalculate and take the report to the board or into your double-materiality or scenario analysis.

    YOUR TEAM
  4. 04

    Check against reality

    In a pilot, the decision becomes a living twin: predictions are frozen and compared with what happens, and the model learns.

    PILOT
